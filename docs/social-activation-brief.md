# Bubble Bolt – Social Activation Brief (Draft)
_Date: 2026-03-23_

## Objectives
- Drive pre-install hype by letting players "feel" the mechanic inside Instagram Stories before app launch.
- Provide influencers with ready-to-use overlays/assets so gameplay footage matches store creatives (per MobileAction best practices).
- Ship weekly evergreen challenge formats that slot into TikTok/Instagram Reels without custom editing.

## Channels & Assets
1. **Instagram Spark AR Filter**
   - Mini-game replicating Round 1: head tilt / swipe to steer a ghost bubble for 10 seconds.
   - Outputs score + CTA "Swipe up to play full game" (deeplink placeholder until launch).
   - Asset needs: simple shader rings, timer UI, branded end card.
2. **Reels/Shorts Toolkit**
   - 3 motion graphic intro/outro bumpers (<2s) with logo + lightning motif.
   - Caption templates emphasizing "Paint the ring in 15s" challenge.
   - Suggested camera framing + speed ramp instructions for creators.
3. **Influencer Kit**
   - B-roll pack (alpha channel) for ghost/aura overlays.
   - Beat sheet: hook (0–3s), gameplay (3–12s), challenge CTA (12–15s).
   - Tracking spreadsheet fields: creator handle, publish date, story vs feed metrics.
4. **UGC Challenge Hashtag**
   - Working hashtag: `#BoltTheRing`.
   - Weekly prompt posts showcasing best score clips; top clips featured in-game on event banners (Phase 2).

## Production Notes
- All assets designed for 9:16 vertical.
- Keep color palette neon cyan/magenta for recognizability against feed clutter.
- Provide localization-ready text layers (EN default) for future markets.

## Next Steps
1. Draft Spark AR logic flow + asset list for handoff.
2. Storyboard first Reel (script + visual references).
3. Compile influencer outreach shortlist (micro + mid-tier) once prototype capture is ready.
